# Real-phone checklist: NuRenaissance site

The tests use a browser at phone sizes, not a real phone. Do these on a real iPhone and an Android if you can.

- [ ] **Bottom bar:** Home, Media, Events, Apps, Services, More all fit on a small phone and each opens the right page.
- [ ] **More sheet:** opens, closes, About works, Refresh works. (Admin link is at the bottom of the footer / More.)
- [ ] **Install:** Add to Home Screen from the site; it opens full-screen and works offline for saved pages.
- [ ] **Apps page:** each card's "Add to Home Screen" shows the right steps for this phone. Certified's opens its install sheet.
- [ ] **Events:** the list loads; a flyer opens its event page; tickets and recap links work; a recap video plays after a tap.
- [ ] **Gallery and Cinema** widgets load, scroll and play inside the page.
- [ ] **Services:** intake form submits (use a test entry), prices load.
- [ ] **Survey:** completes and shows the thank-you; the e-mail arrives.
- [ ] **Notifications** (once the keys are set): footer > Notifications > Turn on; /admin > Phone notifications > send a test; it arrives with the site closed.
- [ ] **Admin on a phone:** sign in, open each folder, the What's new back button returns to Admin, no sideways scrolling.
- [ ] **Update bar:** after a new version, "A new version is ready" shows; tap it.
- [ ] **Offline:** open Home, Events, Gallery and Services once online, then airplane mode and reopen from the Home Screen icon. Pages open; Events, Gallery and Services still list what they last showed. Flyers and videos you had not opened do not load (expected).
